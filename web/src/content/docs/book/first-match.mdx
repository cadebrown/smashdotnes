### Start a match

Open [Play](/play/), click the game for keyboard focus, then choose a title option with **W / S** and press **Enter** or **J**. In the lobby, choose the first fighter with **A / D**. In a CPU mode, choose the second with **W / S**. Press **Enter** or **J** to begin.

<AnnotatedScreenshot scene="lobby" />

Choose **P1 VS AI** for a first game. Move with **A / D**, jump with **W**, jab with **J**, and use a special with **K**.

### Read the battle

<AnnotatedScreenshot scene="battle" />

The percentage below each name is accumulated damage: higher damage sends a fighter farther when hit. Leave an edge, turn back, and use your jumps to return.

Press **Tab** to leave the player. In the lobby, **Select** returns to the title; in battle, hold **Start + Select**. Continue with [controls](/book/#controls), [fighters](/book/#fighters), or [combat and recovery](/book/#combat).
